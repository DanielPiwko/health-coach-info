Privacy & data use

Privacy notice

Updated 11 November 2026

Health Coach is operated by the owner of the DanielPiwko GitHub account for private personal use. It is not a public account service and is not a medical service.

Purpose & information used

The purpose is private review of training, activity, sleep, recovery and body-measurement trends to support personal fitness decisions. Device outputs and interpretations involve estimates and uncertainty, not medical diagnosis or treatment.

  • Hevy: strength-workout history.
  • Withings: weight and body-composition estimates.
  • Google Health: steps, sleep summaries, resting heart rate and heart-rate variability.
  • Personal context: operator-provided training and health context.

Read-only access, limited purpose

Source access is read-only. Health Coach does not edit source measurements, workouts or meals. Connections authorize data reads; broad credentials do not grant permission to make changes in connected services. User information is used only for disclosed personal fitness features, under Google's Health API policy and Limited Use rules. There is no sale, advertising, sharing with data brokers, or insurance, credit or employment use.

Selected summaries & AI

With operator authorization, selected, relevant, minimized summaries may be shared with OpenAI, the current AI provider, for personal fitness assistance. Entire source archives are not automatically sent. Summaries exclude credentials, provider account IDs, full birth date, location tracks and unnecessary free-text workout notes.

Health Coach does not itself train models. AI provider processing and retention are also governed by provider terms and operator account controls. The provider account's retention and training settings are not independently verified. No automatic AI coaching or review schedule is currently enabled.

Private storage & retention

Raw records remain on operator-controlled private storage, on an encrypted host volume with owner-only file access. These permissions do not isolate other processes running as the same user.

Files, history, diagnostic copies and generated summaries may remain until the operator manually deletes them. There is no fixed automatic retention period, no automatic purge timer and no self-service deletion endpoint.

Disconnecting & deletion

The operator can disconnect a source, revoke source permissions, and manually delete local records and saved reports. Privacy or deletion requests can also be sent to the contact below. Revocation stops future reads; it does not automatically delete previously collected data or AI-provider copies. Copies already processed by the AI provider remain subject to its terms, retention practices and available account controls.

Public website & contact

GitHub Pages is the intended host for this public information site, not health records. The public pages do not receive connected fitness records. Reading this site does not create an account or connect a fitness source. Hosting may process normal web request metadata under GitHub's privacy statement. There are no app-added analytics or cookies.

For privacy or deletion questions, contact danielpiwko@gmail.com. Messages are handled by the operator and email provider. Do not email passwords, tokens or unnecessary sensitive records.
